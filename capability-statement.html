Depri at a glance

Grounded in mining. Focused on delivery.

Depri Projects CC is a South African, service-oriented mining company supplying aggregates, granite, gravel, marble stone, river sand and building sand, with a Limpopo project focus and a commercial approach built around practical execution, clear communication and dependable delivery. We also provide agricultural services to support farming productivity.
