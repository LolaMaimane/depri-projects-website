About Depri

Grounded in mining. Focused on delivery.

Depri Projects CC is a service-oriented company specialising in the supply of quality aggregates and granite, gravel, marble stone, river sand and building sand, as well as the provision of agricultural services.

Our aim is to provide reliable, cost-effective and efficient solutions for customers across the construction, building and agricultural sectors, and to build long-term relationships through professionalism and reliability.
