02 / About

Grounded in mining. Focused on delivery.

Depri Projects CC is a service-oriented company specialising in the supply of quality aggregates and granite, gravel, marble stone, river sand and building sand, as well as the provision of agricultural services. Our aim is to provide reliable, cost-effective and efficient solutions that meet the needs of customers across the construction, building and agricultural sectors.

We are committed to delivering quality products and dependable services while building long-term relationships through professionalism, reliability and customer satisfaction. Our goal is to be a trusted partner in construction and agriculture by providing products and services that contribute to sustainable development and business growth.

10 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What materials does Depri Projects supply?

We supply aggregates and crushed stone, granite, gravel, marble stone, river sand, and building and plaster sand, in bulk and delivered to site.

Do you deliver to construction sites?

Yes. We offer bulk transportation and scheduled deliveries of aggregates, sand, granite, gravel and marble stone to construction sites and customer premises.

Where is your mining operation?

Our aggregate project is at Farm Locatie Van De Knopneuzen 230 LT in Limpopo. Our head office is in Glen Marais, Kempton Park, Gauteng.

Do you respond to tenders and RFQs?

Yes. We pursue opportunities through tenders, contracts, partnerships and supplier relationships. Choose "Tender / RFQ" in the enquiry form below.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We welcome international enquiries, particularly for granite and marble stone. Supporting credentials are available to qualified clients, subject to confidentiality and approval.

What agricultural services do you offer?

Land preparation and ploughing, tractor and machinery hire, planting and harvesting support, farm maintenance and transport of agricultural products.
