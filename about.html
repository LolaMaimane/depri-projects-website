About Depri Projects

Grounded in mining. Focused on delivery.

A South African company supplying construction materials and agricultural services since 2002.

Who we are

A trusted partner in construction and agriculture.

Depri Projects CC is a service-oriented company specialising in the supply of quality aggregates and granite, gravel, marble stone, river sand and building sand, as well as the provision of agricultural services. Our aim is to provide reliable, cost-effective and efficient solutions that meet the needs of customers across the construction, building and agricultural sectors.

We are committed to delivering quality products and dependable services while building long-term relationships through professionalism, reliability and customer satisfaction. Our goal is to be a trusted partner in construction and agriculture by providing products and services that contribute to sustainable development and business growth.

Our Mission

Value through quality.

To provide high-quality construction materials, including aggregates, granite, gravel, marble stone, river sand and building sand, together with reliable agricultural services that meet our customers' needs. We deliver value through quality products, dependable service, competitive pricing and strong customer relationships while contributing to infrastructure development and agricultural growth.

Our Vision

A trusted, leading provider.

To become a trusted and leading provider of construction materials and agricultural services, recognised for quality, reliability, customer satisfaction and sustainable business practices, while contributing to the development of communities and the growth of the construction and agricultural sectors.
